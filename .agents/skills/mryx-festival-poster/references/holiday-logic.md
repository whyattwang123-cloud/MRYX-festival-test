# Holiday / Solar-term Logic

## A. Holiday posters
Typical holidays:
- Spring Festival
- Lantern Festival
- Women's Day
- Mother's Day
- 520
- Dragon Boat Festival
- Qixi
- Mid-Autumn Festival
- National Day
- Double 11
- Christmas
- New Year

### Typical visual behavior
- stronger atmosphere
- clearer occasion recognition
- more gift-like mood when appropriate
- product often acts as one of the focal anchors
- can use ceremony cues, but remain premium and restrained

### Color tendencies by type
- Spring Festival / Mid-Autumn / Double 11: red, warm ivory, light gold, warm beige
- Women's Day / Mother's Day: elegant warm neutrals, soft pink accents only if tasteful
- Christmas / New Year: festive but refined, avoid tacky holiday stock clichés

## B. Solar-term posters
Typical solar terms:
- Beginning of Spring
- Spring Equinox
- Qingming
- Beginning of Summer
- Grain Buds
- Summer Solstice
- Beginning of Autumn
- White Dew
- Autumn Equinox
- Beginning of Winter
- Heavy Snow
- Winter Solstice

### Typical visual behavior
- lighter and more atmospheric
- more breathing room
- more natural cues and seasonal micro-details
- less sales pressure
- product may be smaller and more integrated

### Example cues
- 立春:嫩芽、微风、浅绿、柔光、清晨感
- 白露:露珠、清晨、清冷浅蓝白、轻雾
- 冬至:温暖、团圆、暖棕或深红、安静节令感

## Real-asset handling
When real product/logo assets will be composited later:
- keep product-display area clean enough
- do not create fake packshot substitutes in the background prompt
- scene elements should frame, not compete with, the fixed assets
