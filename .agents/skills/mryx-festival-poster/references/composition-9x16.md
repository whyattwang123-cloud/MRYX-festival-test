# 9:16 Composition Library

Default poster orientation: vertical 9:16.

## C01 Upper Copy / Lower Product
Best for:
- most holiday posters
- solar-term posters
- when later typography is required

Structure:
- upper 30–40%: title-safe area
- middle 20–30%: atmosphere transition area
- lower 30–40%: product-display area / support plane

Notes:
- stable and versatile
- ideal when using fixed product PNGs later

## C02 Middle Hero / Breathing Top and Bottom
Best for:
- more premium brand-KV feel
- stronger scene atmosphere

Structure:
- top: light safe space
- middle: hero scene focus
- lower: support plane or platform

Notes:
- more visual elegance
- should still preserve enough clean product placement room

## C03 Upper-left Copy / Lower-right Product
Best for:
- gift-giving holiday feel
- diagonal flow and stronger commercial dynamism

Structure:
- upper-left: logo/title-safe cluster
- center: atmosphere and visual transition
- lower-right: fixed product compositing zone

Notes:
- commercial and directional
- useful when multiple assets need hierarchy

## Composition rules for fixed assets
When real packshots will be composited:
- keep at least one clean insertion area
- avoid branches, rocks, flowers, or bright highlights intersecting the product placement area
- keep the logo zone visually calmer than the decorative edges
- let scene depth support, not dominate, the product
