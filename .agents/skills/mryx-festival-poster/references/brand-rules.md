# Brand Rules — 每日悦享节日/节气海报

## Brand tone
Preferred:
- premium
- warm
- clean
- giftable
- optimistic
- refined
- restrained
- brand-oriented

Avoid:
- noisy promotional graphics
- cheap e-commerce sale aesthetics
- dense particles and confetti
- excessive gold-metal stage design
- fake bloom / overexposed glow
- childish or immature illustration styles
- random China-style clichés piled together

## Asset philosophy
Logo and product packshots are treated as **fixed brand assets**.
They should be used as locked materials, not redrawn.

### For logo assets
- preserve shape, typography, color relationship, and proportions
- place in clean safe zones
- avoid distortion, perspective warp, bevel, or fake 3D treatment

### For product assets
- preserve packaging structure
- preserve label hierarchy and proportions
- preserve color and pack details
- do not cover critical information excessively
- integrate with believable contact shadow and subtle environment reflection only when needed

## Holiday / solar-term atmosphere
The scene should support the brand rather than overpower it.
The image should feel suitable for later typography placement.

## Title-safe thinking
Always prioritize readable upper or upper-middle text space.
Do not clutter the main text area with dense branches, bright highlights, or competing props.

## Product-display area
The lower-middle or lower section should usually contain a calm display area or visual support plane.
Keep it usable for later asset compositing.
